This manual provides information about the test development process and provides evidence that HackerRank’s assessments are highly job-related, reliable, and fair to all test-takers, regardless of their gender, ethnicity, or socio-economic background.

To know about this, refer to the guide [Development, Validation, and Fairness of HackerRank Pre-Employment Technical Skills Assessments.](<https://drive.google.com/file/d/1k--UdK03eG_ffXjlqK_ActzudYVva4ha/view>)
