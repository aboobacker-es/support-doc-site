You can schedule a session with a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) to get expert guidance on using HackerRank.

HackerRank’s FDE team is available to help you with standard tasks such as:

  * How to assemble tests and follow best practices

  * How to search and navigate the HackerRank library

  * How to upload custom questions to HackerRank

  * How to review and understand assessment results

  * How to manage and control access to resources

  * Any other questions or issues you may have

Use the links below to schedule a session with an FDE based on your region:
