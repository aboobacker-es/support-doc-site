You can cancel your subscription if you no longer wish to use HackerRank for Work.

## Cancelling subscription

To cancel your subscription:

  1. Log in to **HackerRank for Work** using your credentials.

  2. Go to **Manage Subscription** from the profile dropdown.

  3. Click **Cancel Plan** under the **Current Plan** section. A confirmation window appears.
