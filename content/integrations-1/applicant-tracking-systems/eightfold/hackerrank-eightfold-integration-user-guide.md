This guide outlines how to use the Eightfold - HackerRank integration to send HackerRank test invitations and review test results directly in Eightfold.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you meet the following requirements:

  * You have active HackerRank and Eightfold accounts.

  * The HackerRank integration with Eightfold is complete. For more information, see [📄 Eightfold - HackerRank Integration Guide](</articles/2732992835>).

## Inviting a candidate to a HackerRank test

You can move a candidate to a stage where you can send a HackerRank test as part of the hiring process.

To invite a candidate to a HackerRank test:

  1. Log in to your Eightfold account using your credentials.

  2. Go to the **Positions** tab.

  3. Select the position for which you want to send a HackerRank assessment.

## Viewing a candidate’s test results

When a candidate completes a HackerRank test, Eightfold displays the test results in the candidate's profile.

To view a candidate’s test results:

  1. Open the candidate's profile in Eightfold.

  2. Go to **Hiring Activities > Assessments**.  
The test report includes the following information:

![image.png](https://assets.usepylon.com/e6a58e21-be80-4777-9eaf-f73beeee94d9%2F1779216072666-image.png?Expires=253370764800&Signature=l5myz3tkwxNSmUUwzHxLfrWj3-qUwFGVS1FihRHhP4kE4XkPsUUG-pGEcBgU-gkNx8RwIG1wnOZ~LsxVAU~O5EQznX43dwwoJ6mWIbgevclRjLpF7zNpzF-APxqDIlaOr2Z0X2oUKbhzo6LV3y3C-7xvjS~uy-yTt8gNGe7LFdKkyHoK0yYuKcSmzdMwTWLURTTzqRsXMuZg85oVQBT8SkIE~zU2dWVYLX5JV5bbPAVEXK9m2M-d9kkNXjpcW7kEQtTJxgzeAptXBygkcftH7XyfwWQxXaoGCfYura-X7qtEG-Cskj4Y-r1gpt~br5GhYGI6speYkNzDiZsxKKb3ZQ__&Key-Pair-Id=K3NV4LZ47N8M46)

  * **Name:** The name of the candidate

  * **Invite link:** The test invite link

  * **Score** : Candidate’s final test score

  * **Plagiarism** : Indicates whether potential plagiarism is detected

  * **Report** : Click **View Report** to open the detailed report in HackerRank

  * **Sent At:** Date and time when the test was sent to the candidate.

  * **Started At** : Date and time when the candidate starts the test.

  * **Finished At** : Date and time when the candidate completes the test.

  * **Comments:** Includes candidate integrity signals:

    * **Integrity Issues:** Indicates whether potential integrity issues are detected.

**Note:** Integrity issues are classified as **None** , **Medium** and **High** based on severity.

    * **Integrity Summary:** Overview of suspicious activities detected during the test.
